Let $F$ be the field of definition of the Legendre curve supplied at $x$, and let $T\subset F$ be its tripodal field. The normalized finite-height identity is
$$
h_P(x)=\frac{h_{\mathrm{fin},\mathbb Q(\lambda)}(J_\lambda)}
              {[\mathbb Q(\lambda):\mathbb Q]},
$$
where $J_\lambda$ is the Legendre $j$-invariant.

The finite-height sum formula identifies $[F:\mathbb Q]\,h_P(x)$ with the finite height in $F$ of the curve's $j$-invariant. This invariant comes from an element of $T$, so the extension formula expresses its finite height in $F$ as $[F:T]$ times the finite height of that element in $T$. The isomorphism $T\simeq\mathbb Q(\lambda)$ carries the element to $J_\lambda$ and preserves its finite height.

The tower law gives $[F:\mathbb Q]=[F:T]\,[\mathbb Q(\lambda):\mathbb Q]$. Both factors are positive. Cancel the relative degree and divide by the absolute degree of the point field.
