Write $\lambda=\mathrm{gen}(x)$ and $d=[\mathbb Q(\lambda):\mathbb Q]$. Let $h$ denote the relative logarithmic height. The provider normalization gives $P.h(x)=h_{\mathrm{fin}}(j(\lambda))/d$, while $H(x)=h(\lambda)/d$. The proof compares $h_{\mathrm{fin}}(j(\lambda))$ with twice the sum of the finite heights of $\lambda$, $\lambda^{-1}$, and $(\lambda-1)^{-1}$. Compact bounds control the finite places over $V$ and the archimedean contributions. Height inversion and the height bound for addition introduce the term $\log(2)/3$. If $j(\lambda)=0$, the proof uses $\lambda^6=1$, so both normalized heights vanish. Division by $d>0$ gives the stated constants without a degree bound.
