Fix an algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ of $\mathbb Q$. For $\lambda\in\overline{\mathbb Q}$, let $E_\lambda$ be the Weierstrass model $Y^2=X(X-1)(X-\lambda)$, and let $E_\lambda(\overline{\mathbb Q})[n]$ be the nonsingular points killed by multiplication by $n$, including the identity. Take any package $P$ certifying finiteness of both $E_\lambda(\overline{\mathbb Q})[3]$ and $E_\lambda(\overline{\mathbb Q})[5]$ for every $\lambda$, including $0$ and $1$.
For each $\lambda\ne0,1$, set $K_\lambda=\mathbb Q(\lambda)$ and let $d_\lambda$ be the positive degree of the minimal polynomial of $\lambda$ over $\mathbb Q$. Let $L_\lambda$ be the number field obtained from $K_\lambda$ by adjoining one chosen square root of each of $-1$, $\lambda$, and $1-\lambda$, and both affine coordinates of every nonidentity point in the groups killed by $3$ and by $5$. Regard $E_\lambda$ as an elliptic curve over $L_\lambda$, whose algebraic closure is $\overline{\mathbb Q}$, and set $M_\lambda=\mathbb Q(j(E_\lambda))\subseteq L_\lambda$.
For a finite place $w$ of $L_\lambda$ use its associated prime ideal $\mathfrak p_w$, its residue characteristic $p_w=\operatorname{char}(\mathcal O_{L_\lambda}/\mathfrak p_w)$, and its residue degree $f_w=[\mathcal O_{L_\lambda}/\mathfrak p_w:\mathbb F_{p_w}]$. Let $B_\lambda$ consist of every finite place $w$ at which some Weierstrass change of variables over $L_\lambda$, followed by base change to the completion $L_{\lambda,w}$, gives a model with multiplicative reduction over the ring of integers of that completion.
For every $\lambda\ne0,1$, $P$ also certifies that $-1$ is a square in $L_\lambda$ and that at each $w$ the curve has good or multiplicative reduction in this sense. Every point in $E_\lambda(\overline{\mathbb Q})[6]$ is the image under base change of an $L_\lambda$-rational point of $E_\lambda$. For each prime $\ell\ge7$, $L_\lambda/M_\lambda$ is Galois and $[L_\lambda:M_\lambda]$ is coprime to $\ell$. Each $w$ lies over a finite place $v$ of $M_\lambda$, where lying over means contraction of the associated prime ideal, and every such pair has equal residue characteristics. If $v$ has residue characteristic other than $2$ and $w,w'$ lie over $v$, then $w\in B_\lambda$ implies $w'\in B_\lambda$. The set $B_\lambda$ is finite, $f_w>0$ for every $w$, and $\sum_{w\in B_\lambda,\ p_w=r} f_w\le [L_\lambda:\mathbb Q]$ for every $r\in\mathbb N$.
For each $\lambda\ne0,1$ and each prime $\ell$, $P$ supplies an additive isomorphism $b:E_\lambda(\overline{\mathbb Q})[\ell]\simeq(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^2$ and a group homomorphism $\rho:\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/L_\lambda)\to\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)$ with open kernel in the Krull topology. For every automorphism $\sigma$ in this Galois group and every point $T$ killed by $\ell$, these data satisfy $b(\sigma T)=\rho(\sigma)b(T)$, with $\sigma$ acting on the coordinates of $T$ and fixing the identity.
At each $w\in B_\lambda$, the construction fixes a Tate parameter $q_w\in L_{\lambda,w}^{\times}$ by classical choice, with $\|q_w\|_w<1$ for which $c_4(T(q_w))^3/\Delta(T(q_w))$ equals the image of $j(E_\lambda)$ in the completion. Here $c_4$ and $\Delta$ are the Weierstrass invariants of $T(q)$, the model $Y^2+XY=X^3-5s_3(q)X-(5s_3(q)+7s_5(q))/12$, with $s_k(q)=\sum_{n\ge1}n^k q^n/(1-q^n)$. The construction also fixes a uniformizer $\pi_w\in L_{\lambda,w}^{\times}$ of the completion by classical choice, with $\|\pi_w\|_w<1$ such that the norms of all nonzero elements of $L_{\lambda,w}$ are integer powers of $\|\pi_w\|_w$. Let $m_w$ be the natural number obtained from the chosen integer $a_w$ satisfying $\|q_w\|_w=\|\pi_w\|_w^{a_w}$. This integer is positive. The order assigned at places outside $B_\lambda$ is zero.
Evaluate $J_\lambda=256(\lambda^2-\lambda+1)^3/(\lambda^2(\lambda-1)^2)$ in $K_\lambda$. Write $\mathcal V_{\mathrm f}(K_\lambda)$ for the finite places of $K_\lambda$. For $v$ in this set, $|\,\cdot\,|_v$ is the real absolute value given by the norm after embedding in the associated adic completion. The sum on the right is the finite-support sum, which has value zero on infinite support. The logarithm is natural, with $\log 0=0$, so its summands are zero when $J_\lambda=0$. An empty $B_\lambda$ gives a zero sum on the left. Then for every $\lambda\ne0,1$ the following equality holds in $\mathbb R$.

$$
\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{w\in B_\lambda}m_w f_w\log p_w}{[L_\lambda:\mathbb Q]}
=
\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{v\in\mathcal V_{\mathrm f}(K_\lambda)}\max\{0,\log |J_\lambda|_v\}}{d_\lambda}.
$$
