## primeUnder

Take any number field $K$, any field $k$, and a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$. Let $\mathcal O_k$ and $\mathcal O_K$ be the rings of elements integral over $\mathbb Z$ in these fields. Write $\iota_{\mathcal O}$ for the restriction of $\iota$ to these rings. For every finite place $w$ of $K$, let $\mathfrak p_w$ be the associated nonzero prime ideal of $\mathcal O_K$. The definition returns $\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_w)$ as a nonzero prime ideal of $\mathcal O_k$, with its primality and nonvanishing included in the construction.

## placeUnder

Take any number fields $k,K$ with a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$. Write $\iota_{\mathcal O}\colon \mathcal O_k\to\mathcal O_K$ for its restriction to the rings of integers. For every finite place $w$ of $K$ with associated prime ideal $\mathfrak p_w$, the definition returns the finite place of $k$ associated to $\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_w)$. This place is the real absolute value obtained from the norm of the completion of $k$ at that prime, pulled back to $k$ by the canonical embedding.

## placeUnder_maximalIdeal

Take any number fields $k,K$ with a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$, and take any finite place $w$ of $K$ with associated prime ideal $\mathfrak p_w\subset\mathcal O_K$. Let $v_0$ be the finite place of $k$ given by pulling back the completion norm at $\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_w)$ to $k$, where $\iota_{\mathcal O}$ is the induced map on rings of integers. The maximal ideal of $\mathcal O_k$ associated to $v_0$ equals the contraction of $\mathfrak p_w$.
$$
\mathfrak p_{v_0}=\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_w).
$$

## liesOver_placeUnder

Take any number fields $k,K$ with a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$, and take any finite place $w$ of $K$ with associated prime ideal $\mathfrak p_w\subset\mathcal O_K$. Let $v_0$ be the finite place of $k$ given by pulling back the completion norm at $\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_w)$ to $k$, where $\iota_{\mathcal O}$ is the induced map on rings of integers. The place $w$ lies over $v_0$, which means that the prime ideal of $\mathcal O_k$ associated to $v_0$ equals the preimage of $\mathfrak p_w$ under $\iota_{\mathcal O}$.

## placeUnder_eq_of_liesOver_place

Take any number fields $k,K$ with a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$. Take any finite places $w$ of $K$ and $v$ of $k$, with associated prime ideals $\mathfrak p_w\subset\mathcal O_K$ and $\mathfrak p_v\subset\mathcal O_k$. Assume that $\mathfrak p_v=\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_w)$, where $\iota_{\mathcal O}$ is the induced map on rings of integers. Then the finite place of $k$ given by pulling back the completion norm at $\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_w)$ to $k$ equals $v$.

## relRamIdx

Take any number fields $k,K$ with a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$. Take any finite places $v$ of $K$ and $w$ of $k$, with associated prime ideals $\mathfrak p_v\subset\mathcal O_K$ and $\mathfrak p_w\subset\mathcal O_k$. Write $\mathfrak p_w\mathcal O_K$ for the ideal generated by the image of $\mathfrak p_w$ under the induced map of rings of integers. The definition returns the largest natural number $n$ with $\mathfrak p_w\mathcal O_K\subseteq\mathfrak p_v^n$, and returns $0$ if there is no largest such $n$. It also returns $0$ when exponent $0$ is the only one that satisfies the inclusion.

## relInertDeg

Take any number fields $k,K$ with a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$. Take any finite places $v$ of $K$ and $w$ of $k$, with associated prime ideals $\mathfrak p_v\subset\mathcal O_K$ and $\mathfrak p_w\subset\mathcal O_k$. If the preimage of $\mathfrak p_v$ under the induced map $\iota_{\mathcal O}\colon\mathcal O_k\to\mathcal O_K$ equals $\mathfrak p_w$, the definition returns the dimension of $\mathcal O_K/\mathfrak p_v$ over $\mathcal O_k/\mathfrak p_w$ for the induced map of residue fields. For every other pair, it returns $0$.

## ramificationIdx'_ne_zero

Take any number fields $k,K$ with a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$. Take any finite places $w$ of $K$ and $v$ of $k$, with associated prime ideals $\mathfrak p_w\subset\mathcal O_K$ and $\mathfrak p_v\subset\mathcal O_k$. Assume $\mathfrak p_v=\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_w)$ for the induced map $\iota_{\mathcal O}$ on rings of integers. Write $\mathfrak p_v\mathcal O_K$ for the ideal generated by the image of $\mathfrak p_v$. Form the natural-number index given by the largest $n$ with $\mathfrak p_v\mathcal O_K\subseteq\mathfrak p_w^n$, using $0$ if no such largest $n$ exists. Under the stated assumption this index is nonzero.

## valuation_algebraMap_eq_pow

Take any number fields $k,K$ with a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$. Take any finite places $w$ of $K$ and $v$ of $k$, with associated prime ideals $\mathfrak p_w\subset\mathcal O_K$ and $\mathfrak p_v\subset\mathcal O_k$. Assume $\mathfrak p_v=\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_w)$ for the induced map $\iota_{\mathcal O}$ on rings of integers. Let $e$ be the largest natural number $n$ with $\mathfrak p_v\mathcal O_K\subseteq\mathfrak p_w^n$, where $\mathfrak p_v\mathcal O_K$ is the ideal generated by the image of $\mathfrak p_v$. Use $e=0$ if the set of such $n$ has no maximum. For either place $a\in\{v,w\}$, write $\nu_a$ for the multiplicative valuation of its prime ideal. Its values are formal powers $t^m$ of a generator $t$ with $m\in\mathbb Z$ and $t^m t^n=t^{m+n}$, together with an absorbing zero. A nonzero field element $z$ has value $\nu_a(z)=t^{-\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak p_a}(z)}$, where $\operatorname{ord}_{\mathfrak p_a}(z)$ is the exponent of $\mathfrak p_a$ in the principal fractional ideal of $z$ in the field of $a$. The value of zero is the absorbing zero. For every $x\in k$, including $x=0$, the following equality holds.
$$
\nu_w(\iota(x))=\nu_v(x)^e.
$$

## relRamIdx_ne_zero

Take any number fields $k,K$ with a specified field embedding $\iota\colon k\to K$. Take any finite places $v$ of $K$ and $w$ of $k$, with associated prime ideals $\mathfrak p_v\subset\mathcal O_K$ and $\mathfrak p_w\subset\mathcal O_k$. Assume $\mathfrak p_w=\iota_{\mathcal O}^{-1}(\mathfrak p_v)$ for the induced map $\iota_{\mathcal O}$ on rings of integers. Define $e(v/w)$ as the greatest natural number $n$ with $\mathfrak p_w\mathcal O_K\subseteq\mathfrak p_v^n$, where $\mathfrak p_w\mathcal O_K$ is the ideal generated by the image of $\mathfrak p_w$. The definition gives $0$ if there is no greatest such $n$. Under the stated assumption, $e(v/w)\ne 0$.
