> Thus, in summary, by replacing X by $\mathbb{P}^1_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}}$, T by the point “0” of $\mathbb{P}^1_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}}$, and S by the union of $\psi(S)$ and the points of $\mathbb{P}^1_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}}$ over which $\psi$ ramifies, we conclude that we may reduce to the case $X=\mathbb{P}^1_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}}$, $T=\{\beta\}$, for some $\beta\in\mathbb{P}^1(\overline{\mathbb{Q}})\backslash\{\infty\}$.

— Shinichi Mochizuki, *Noncritical Belyi Maps*, proof of Theorem 2.5, printed p.6.
