## deg_mul_height

Let $I$ be any index type, $S$ a finite subset of $I$, $p,a,f$ functions from $I$ to $\mathbb N$, and $d\in\mathbb N$ with $d\ge 1$. Assume that for every $v\in S$, $p(v)$ is prime, $a(v)\ge 1$, and $f(v)\ge 1$. Assume also that for every $r\in\mathbb N$, $\sum_{v\in S,\ p(v)=r}f(v)\le d$. Define $H$ to be $\bigl(\sum_{v\in S}a(v)f(v)\log p(v)\bigr)/d$, where $\log$ is the natural logarithm and the natural numbers in this expression are viewed as real numbers. The set $S$ may be empty, in which case $H=0$. For every such input, the following real equality holds.
$$
dH=\sum_{v\in S}a(v)f(v)\log p(v).
$$

## finrank_mul_h_eq_finHeight

Let $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ be an algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$, and let $E_\lambda$ denote the Legendre Weierstrass model $Y^2=X(X-1)(X-\lambda)$. Its point group over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ consists of the nonsingular affine points and the point at infinity. The input $P$ supplies finiteness, for every $\lambda\in\overline{\mathbb Q}$ including $0$ and $1$, of each of the sets of points in the point group of $E_\lambda$ killed by $3$ and by $5$. For each $y\in\overline{\mathbb Q}\setminus\{0,1\}$, let $F_y$ be the subfield of $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ generated over $\mathbb Q$ by $y$, the chosen square roots of $-1$, $y$, and $1-y$, and both coordinates of each affine point of $E_y$ over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ killed by $3$ or by $5$. Regard $F_y$ as a number field with algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ and $E_y$ as the elliptic curve over $F_y$ given by that model. Write $j_y=j(E_y)$, $d_y=[F_y:\mathbb Q]$, and $K_y=\mathbb Q(j_y)\subseteq F_y$. Write $M_F^0$ for the set of finite places of a number field $F$. For $w\in M_F^0$, $|\cdot|_w$ is the absolute value obtained by pulling back the norm from the adic completion at the corresponding nonzero prime ideal $\mathfrak m_w$ of the ring of integers $\mathcal O_F$. Write $p(w)$ for the characteristic of $\mathcal O_F/\mathfrak m_w$ and $f(w)$ for its degree over $\mathbb F_{p(w)}$, using the field belonging to the place in each case. Write $S_y$ for the set of finite places of $F_y$ at which $E_y$ has multiplicative reduction. Here good or multiplicative reduction at a place means there exists a Weierstrass change of variables over $F_y$ after which the model, extended to the completion at the place, has the indicated reduction over the ring of integers of that completion. For every such $y$, $P$ supplies that $-1$ is a square in $F_y$, that the curve has good or multiplicative reduction at every finite place, and that every point of $E_y$ over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ killed by $6$ is the image of a point over $F_y$ under base change. For each prime $\ell\ge 7$, the extension $F_y/K_y$ is Galois and its degree is coprime to $\ell$. Every finite place $w$ of $F_y$ lies over some finite place $v$ of $K_y$, and $p(w)=p(v)$ whenever $w$ lies over $v$. For each finite place $v$ of $K_y$ with $p(v)\ne 2$, membership in $S_y$ of one place above $v$ implies membership in $S_y$ of every place above $v$. The set $S_y$ is finite, $f(w)>0$ at every finite place $w$ of $F_y$, and for every $r\in\mathbb N$ one has $\sum_{w\in S_y,\ p(w)=r}f(w)\le d_y$. The input $P$ also supplies, for each such $y$ and every prime $\ell$, an additive group isomorphism $\beta$ from the points of $E_y$ over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ killed by $\ell$ to $(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^2$, and a group homomorphism $\rho\colon\operatorname{Aut}_{F_y}(\overline{\mathbb Q})\to\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)$ whose kernel is open in the automorphism group topology. For every such point $T$ and every such automorphism $\sigma$, $\beta(\sigma T)=\rho(\sigma)\beta(T)$, where $\sigma$ acts on the coordinates of the point. Given any such $P$ and any $x\in\overline{\mathbb Q}\setminus\{0,1\}$, define $h_P(x)=d_x^{-1}\sum_{w\in S_x}a(w)f(w)\log p(w)$. For each $w\in S_x$, a nonzero Tate parameter $q_w$ of norm strictly below $1$ is chosen in the completion at $w$ with Tate $j$-invariant equal to the image of $j_x$ in that completion. A nonzero uniformizer $\pi_w$ of norm strictly below $1$ is chosen there, with every nonzero element of the completion having norm $\|\pi_w\|^n$ for some $n\in\mathbb Z$. The local value $a(w)$ is the positive integer satisfying $\|q_w\|=\|\pi_w\|^{a(w)}$, and $a(w)=0$ outside $S_x$. Natural numbers in real expressions are viewed as real numbers. The sum $\sum^{\mathrm f}$ below sums the nonzero terms when there are finitely many and has value $0$ when there are infinitely many. It uses the convention $\log 0=0$, so $j_x=0$ gives zero terms. If $S_x$ is empty, the left-hand side is zero and the equality requires the right-hand side to be zero. The assertion is the real equality
$$
d_x h_P(x)=\sum_{w\in M_{F_x}^0}^{\mathrm f}\max\{0,\log|j_x|_w\}.
$$
