> Now let us set
>
> $$
> \epsilon_E\overset{\mathrm{def}}{=}(60\delta)^2\cdot h^{-1/2}\cdot\log(2\delta\cdot h)\quad(\geq 5\cdot\delta\cdot h^{-1/2});
> $$

> $$
> \epsilon_d^*\overset{\mathrm{def}}{=}\frac{1}{16}\cdot\epsilon_d\quad(<\tfrac{1}{2}\leq 1)
