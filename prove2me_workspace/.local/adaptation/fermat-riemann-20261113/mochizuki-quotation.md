> “Since $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ is infinite, it thus follows that there exists an $s_1\in\Gamma(X,\mathcal{L})$ such that $s_1(t)\ne0$ for all $t\in T$. Thus, the linear series determined by the sections $s_0,s_1$ of $\mathcal{L}$ has no basepoints, hence determines a finite morphism $\psi:X\to\mathbb{P}^1_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}}$.”

— Shinichi Mochizuki, *Noncritical Belyi Maps*, proof of Theorem 2.5, printed p. 6.
