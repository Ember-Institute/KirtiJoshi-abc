## Superrigid

设 $k$ 为带绝对值的局部紧域，具有可判定相等性。写 $B=\mathbb R\mathbb P^1$、$Y=\mathbb P^1(k)$，$a$ 为用可逆矩阵表示的射影作用，$\mathrm{bdry}$ 为 $B$ 的边界测度。`Equivariant Γ a ψ` 指点映射 $\psi:B\to Y$ 对每个 $\gamma\in\Gamma$ 满足 $\psi(\gamma b)=a(\gamma)\psi(b)$ 对 $\mathrm{bdry}$ 几乎处处的 $b$ 成立。`ActsOn Γ a` 要求对 $\Gamma$ 中每对元素 $g,h$ 和每个 $y\in Y$，$a(gh)y=a(g)(a(h)y)$。`NonElem Γ a` 要求 $\Gamma$ 的每个有限指数子群在 $Y$ 上都没有公共不动点。对满足双遍历性的可数 $\Gamma$，给定 `ActsOn Γ a` 与 `NonElem Γ a`，`ae_eq_of_equivariant` 断言两条可测同变点映射几乎处处相等。若 $\Gamma\le\Delta\le\mathrm{Comm}(\Gamma)$，$\Gamma$ 可数且其每个有限指数子群均双遍历，并给定 `NonElem Γ a` 与 `ActsOn Δ a`，`equivariant_commensurator` 将可测 $\Gamma$ 同变点映射的同变性推广到 $\Delta$。

若 $\Delta$ 在 $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ 中稠密，$\psi$ 可测且对 $\Delta$ 同变，且 $\psi(b_1)\ne\psi(b_2)$ 对乘积边界测度几乎处处成立，则 `exists_gl_of_dense` 对每个 $g\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ 给出一个矩阵 $M\in\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$，使 $\psi(gb)=M\psi(b)$ 几乎处处成立。在同一组前提下，`exists_extension` 给出 $\Phi:\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)\to\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$。对每个 $y\in Y$，有 $\Phi(gh)y=\Phi(g)(\Phi(h)y)$，对 $\delta\in\Delta$ 有 $\Phi(\delta)y=a(\delta)y$，且 $g\mapsto\Phi(g)y$ 连续。上游 `commensurator_superrigidity` 用同变性推广和这个延拓结论得到全群的射影作用。

Let $k$ be a field with a proper norm, and assume decidable equality on $k$. Write $B=\mathbb R\mathbb P^1$ and $Y=\mathbb P^1(k)$. The map $a$ represents projective transformations by invertible matrices, and $\mathrm{bdry}$ is the boundary measure on $B$. For a point map $\psi:B\to Y$, equivariance means $\psi(\gamma b)=a(\gamma)\psi(b)$ for $\mathrm{bdry}$-almost every $b$, for each $\gamma\in\Gamma$. The predicate `ActsOn Γ a` requires $a(gh)y=a(g)(a(h)y)$ for each $g,h\in\Gamma$ and $y\in Y$. The predicate `NonElem Γ a` requires every finite-index subgroup of $\Gamma$ to have no common fixed point in $Y$. Assume $\Gamma$ is countable and doubly ergodic, and assume `ActsOn Γ a` and `NonElem Γ a`. Then `ae_eq_of_equivariant` gives almost-everywhere equality of two measurable equivariant point maps.

Suppose $\Gamma\le\Delta\le\mathrm{Comm}(\Gamma)$ and $\Gamma$ is countable. Assume that each finite-index subgroup of $\Gamma$ is doubly ergodic. With `NonElem Γ a` and `ActsOn Δ a`, `equivariant_commensurator` gives $\Delta$-equivariance of a measurable $\Gamma$-equivariant point map.

Suppose $\Delta$ is dense in $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ and $\psi$ is measurable and $\Delta$-equivariant. Assume $\psi(b_1)\ne\psi(b_2)$ for almost every pair under the product boundary measure. For each $g\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$, `exists_gl_of_dense` gives $M\in\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$ with $\psi(gb)=M\psi(b)$ almost everywhere. Under the same hypotheses, `exists_extension` gives $\Phi:\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)\to\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$. For each $y\in Y$, it satisfies $\Phi(gh)y=\Phi(g)(\Phi(h)y)$ and $\Phi(\delta)y=a(\delta)y$ for $\delta\in\Delta$. Also, $g\mapsto\Phi(g)y$ is continuous for each $y\in Y$. The upstream theorem `commensurator_superrigidity` uses both the extension of equivariance and `exists_extension` to obtain a projective action of the full group.

## Selection

取可测空间 $X$、非空且带博雷尔结构的拓扑空间 $Z$，以及可测集合 $S\subseteq X$。对核 $\rho$，若每个 $x\in S$ 都有 $z\in Z$ 和非零 $c\in[0,\infty]$ 使 $\rho(x)=c\,\delta_z$，则 `exists_measurable_dirac_selection` 给出定义于整个 $X$ 的可测映射 $f:X\to Z$。对每个 $x\in S$，仍存在非零 $c\in[0,\infty]$ 使 $\rho(x)=c\,\delta_{f(x)}$。若 $Z$ 的每个单点集都可测，且马尔可夫核 $\kappa$ 在每个 $x\in S$ 均有质量严格大于 $1/2$ 的原子，则 `exists_measurable_heavy_selection` 给出可测 $f:X\to Z$，使 $\kappa(x)\{f(x)\}>1/2$ 对每个 $x\in S$ 成立。它使用 `mem_iff_of_heavy`，该引理对概率测度 $\mu$ 和满足 $\mu\{z\}>1/2$ 的 $z$ 给出 $z\in U$ 当且仅当 $\mu(U)>1/2$，其中 $U\subseteq Z$ 可任取。`FromProb` 用第二个选择定理选取重原子，在两点各有质量 $1/2$ 的分支用第一个定理从受限二重积核中选取有序点对。

Let $X$ be a measurable space and $Z$ a nonempty topological space with its Borel structure. Let $S\subseteq X$ be measurable. Suppose each $x\in S$ has a $z\in Z$ and a nonzero $c\in[0,\infty]$ with $\rho(x)=c\,\delta_z$ for the kernel $\rho$. Then `exists_measurable_dirac_selection` gives a measurable $f:X\to Z$. For each $x\in S$, some nonzero $c\in[0,\infty]$ satisfies $\rho(x)=c\,\delta_{f(x)}$.

Assume all singleton sets in $Z$ are measurable. If the Markov kernel $\kappa$ has an atom of mass strictly greater than $1/2$ at each $x\in S$, `exists_measurable_heavy_selection` selects one measurably. It gives $f:X\to Z$ with $\kappa(x)\{f(x)\}>1/2$ for each $x\in S$. For a probability measure $\mu$ with $\mu\{z\}>1/2$, `mem_iff_of_heavy` gives $z\in U$ if and only if $\mu(U)>1/2$ for every $U\subseteq Z$. `FromProb` uses heavy-atom selection when such an atom exists. When both points have mass $1/2$, it uses Dirac selection on a restricted double product to choose an ordered pair.
