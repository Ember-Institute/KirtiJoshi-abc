## English

Fix a tripod point $x$ with parameter $\lambda=x.1$. Given finiteness of the geometric $3$- and $5$-torsion, `curveOf` gives the Legendre curve $E_\lambda/F_\lambda$ with chosen algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}$. The field $F_\lambda$ is generated over $\mathbb Q$ by $\lambda$, the chosen square roots of $-1$, $\lambda$ and $1-\lambda$, and the coordinates of the $3$- and $5$-torsion points. The helper `curveOf_baseChange` identifies the curve after base change to $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ with the Legendre model at $\lambda$ there. It applies `legendre_baseChange` to the element representing $\lambda$ in $F_\lambda$. [Curve and field](../../../../Definitions/Def_AbcAdaptation_Reference_Iut_Tripod_CurveOf.lean#L25) · [Base change](../../../../Solutions/Source_TripodProviderRepresentation.lean#L21)

For each prime $\ell$, `modRep_curveOf` uses that equality to apply `legendre_torsionBasis` to the geometric $\ell$-torsion of `curveOf`. The constructor `IutCurveModEllRepData.ofBasis` chooses the additive equivalence $b\colon E_\lambda[\ell](\overline{\mathbb Q})\simeq(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^2$ and expresses the Galois action in this basis. The resulting representation $\rho_\ell\colon\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/F_\lambda)\to\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)$ satisfies $b(\sigma Q)=\rho_\ell(\sigma)b(Q)$ for each geometric $\ell$-torsion point $Q$. Its kernel is open in the Krull topology. The proof uses the finite intersection of the open stabilizers of the torsion points. The stabilizer of an affine point is the intersection of the stabilizers of its two coordinates. [Torsion basis](../../../../Solutions/Source_TripodTorsionBasis.lean#L26) · [Adapted representation](../../../../Solutions/Source_TripodProviderRepresentation.lean#L28) · [Open kernel](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/IutInitialThetaModEllRepConstruct.lean#L156) · [Basis constructor](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/IutInitialThetaModEllRepConstruct.lean#L174) · [Reference constructor](https://github.com/lana-agents/iut/blob/10e40d2e52a8e7220c2a90ff1595f5b385f987ef/Iut/Concrete/ModEllRepConstruct.lean#L173)

The caller of `CurveProviders.ofArithmetic` explicitly supplies `arith`, a proof of `CurveArithmetic` for the constructed curve at every tripod point. This includes stable reduction at every finite place, rationality of the $6$-torsion, and the condition that $F_\lambda/F_{\mathrm{mod}}$ is Galois of degree prime to every prime $\ell\ge7$. The constructor uses `legendre_torsionFinite` at $3$ and $5$ for the two finiteness fields, installs `arith` as given, and uses `modRep_curveOf` for the representation field. The reference `tripodProviders` fills its arithmetic field with `CurveArithmetic.ofCore` and the corresponding arithmetic theorems. ## 中文

对每个素数 $\ell$，`modRep_curveOf` 用上述等式，对 `curveOf` 的几何 $\ell$-挠点应用 `legendre_torsionBasis`。构造函数 `IutCurveModEllRepData.ofBasis` 选取加法同构 $b\colon E_\lambda[\ell](\overline{\mathbb Q})\simeq(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^2$，并用这组基表示 Galois 作用。所得表示 $\rho_\ell\colon\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/F_\lambda)\to\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)$ 对每个几何 $\ell$-挠点 $Q$ 满足 $b(\sigma Q)=\rho_\ell(\sigma)b(Q)$。其核在 Krull 拓扑中是开的。证明使用挠点的开稳定子作有限交。仿射点的稳定子是两个坐标的稳定子的交。 [挠点基](../../../../Solutions/Source_TripodTorsionBasis.lean#L26) · [适配表示](../../../../Solutions/Source_TripodProviderRepresentation.lean#L28) · [核的开性](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/IutInitialThetaModEllRepConstruct.lean#L156) · [从基构造表示](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/IutInitialThetaModEllRepConstruct.lean#L174) · [参考构造](https://github.com/lana-agents/iut/blob/10e40d2e52a8e7220c2a90ff1595f5b385f987ef/Iut/Concrete/ModEllRepConstruct.lean#L173)
