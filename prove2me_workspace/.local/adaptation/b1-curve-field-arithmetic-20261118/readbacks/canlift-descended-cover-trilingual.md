## English

Let $k$ have characteristic zero and $t$ be transcendental over $k$. Write $N_k=k(t)(y)$, and let $R_F$ be the integral closure of $k[t]$ in a finite extension $F/k(t)$ containing $N_k$. Suppose every maximal ideal of $R_F$ has ramification index $1$ over the normalization in $N_k$. For a $k$-algebra map $f:k[t]\to R_F$, write $e_f(w)$ for the ramification index along $f$. The condition `QuadraticCoverRigidity k t y` requires each injective, integral $f$ to be the canonical inclusion if $e_f(w)=m_k(f^{-1}w)$ at every maximal ideal $w$. Here $m_k$ is the sign quotient multiplicity on $k[t]$. Take a tower $k\subseteq K\subseteq\Omega$ of characteristic zero with $k$ and $\Omega$ algebraically closed and $t$ transcendental over $K$. Let $y^2+by=c$ with $b,c\in k(t)$. Take $F/k(t)$ and $L/K(t)$ finite inside $\Omega$, with $L=K(t)(F)$ and $y\in F$. Write $N_K=K(t)(y)\subseteq L$ and $R_L$ for the integral closure of $K[t]$ in $L$. Assume that the ramification index over the normalization in $N_K$ is $1$ at every maximal ideal of $R_L$. Let $g:K[t]\to R_L$ be an integral $K$-algebra map satisfying $e_g(w)=m_K(g^{-1}w)$ at every maximal ideal $w$ of $R_L$. Put $s=g(t)$, viewed in $\Omega$. If $s\in F$ and rigidity holds over $k$, `quadraticCoverMap_eq_of_descended` gives the equality of $g$ with the canonical inclusion $K[t]\hookrightarrow R_L$. Apply `etale_of_constantExtension` with all four multiplicity functions equal to $1$. This gives index $1$ over the normalization in $N_k$ at every maximal ideal of $R_F$. Since $s\in R_L$, it is integral over $K[t]$. The integrality of $g$ gives $t$ integral over $K[s]$, which also makes $s$ transcendental over $K$. Finiteness of $F/k(t)$ makes $s$ algebraic over $k(t)$, so integrality descends to $k[t]$. Algebraic exchange makes $t$ algebraic over $k(s)$. Apply integrality descent again to show that $t$ is integral over $k[s]$. This gives the parameter maps $t\mapsto s$ over both fields. Agreement on $t$ identifies $g$ with the upper parameter map.
