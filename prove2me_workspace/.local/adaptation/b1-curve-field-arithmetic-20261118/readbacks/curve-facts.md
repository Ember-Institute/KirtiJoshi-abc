## `SL2ImageHyp`

### English

Fix the algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ of $\mathbb Q$. For $t\in\overline{\mathbb Q}$, consider the group of nonsingular affine points and the point at infinity $O$ of $Y^2=X^3-(1+t)X^2+tX$ over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$. For $n=3,5$, let $C_n(t)$ be the union of the sets $\{a,b\}$ from affine points $(a,b)$ killed by multiplication by $n$, with $O$ contributing the empty set. Take a curve-provider record $P$ that certifies finiteness of both groups of points killed by $3$ and by $5$ for every $t$, including $0$ and $1$. A point $x$ under consideration has underlying element $\lambda\in\overline{\mathbb Q}$ with $\lambda\ne0,1$. Its field $F\subseteq\overline{\mathbb Q}$ is generated over $\mathbb Q$ by $\lambda$, the chosen square roots of $-1,\lambda,1-\lambda$, and $C_3(\lambda)\cup C_5(\lambda)$. The field is finite over $\mathbb Q$ by the supplied finiteness. Its elliptic curve $E/F$ is given by the same equation with $t=\lambda$ in $F$, and its chosen algebraic closure is $\overline F=\overline{\mathbb Q}$. For each such $x$, $P$ supplies the following arithmetic assertions. Let $M=\mathbb Q(j(E))\subseteq F$, with $j(E)$ the $j$-invariant. The element $-1$ is a square in $F$. At each finite place of $F$, $E$ has good or multiplicative reduction, tested after a Weierstrass change of variables over $F$ and base change to the completion and its ring of integers. Every point of $E(\overline F)$ killed by $6$ comes from an $F$-point. The extension $F/M$ is Galois of degree coprime to $\ell$ for every prime $\ell\ge7$. Every finite place of $F$ lies over a finite place of $M$, and any such pair has the same residue characteristic. For each finite place $v$ of $M$ of residue characteristic different from $2$, multiplicative reduction at one place of $F$ over $v$ implies multiplicative reduction at every place of $F$ over $v$. The set $\mathcal B$ of finite places of $F$ with multiplicative reduction is finite, each finite place has positive inertia degree over $\mathbb Q$, and for every natural number $q$ the sum of inertia degrees of the places in $\mathcal B$ of residue characteristic $q$ is at most $[F:\mathbb Q]$. At each $w\in\mathcal B$, the construction chooses a nonzero Tate parameter $q_w$ of norm less than $1$ in the completion $F_w$ whose Tate $j$-invariant is the image of $j(E)$, and a uniformizer $\pi_w$ of $F_w$. The uniformizer is nonzero, with norm less than $1$, and the norms of nonzero elements of $F_w$ are its norm's integer powers. Write $m(w)$ for the natural-number order determined by $\|q_w\|_w=\|\pi_w\|_w^{m(w)}$, where $\|\cdot\|_w$ is the completion norm. Write $p(w)$ for the characteristic of the residue field of $w$. For each such $x$ and each natural number $\ell$ with a proof of primality, $P$ supplies a mod-$\ell$ representation. The data consist of an additive identification of the points of $E(\overline F)$ killed by $\ell$ with $(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^2$ and a group homomorphism $\rho$ from the automorphisms of $\overline F$ fixing $F$ to $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)$ with open kernel. For every such automorphism its action on these points is given by multiplying their identified vectors by its matrix under $\rho$. Here the subgroup condition for $\ell$ means that there is an additive subgroup $H\subseteq E(\overline F)$ of cardinality $\ell$ such that each automorphism of $\overline F$ fixing $F$ sends each point of $H$ into $H$. The action applies the automorphism to affine coordinates and fixes $O$. The defined proposition says that for every such $x$, natural number $\ell$, and primality witness, if $\ell\ge5$, if $\ell$ divides none of the $m(w)$ for $w\in\mathcal B$, if the subgroup condition fails, and if there exists $w\in\mathcal B$ with $p(w)\ne2$ and $p(w)\ne\ell$, then every determinant-one $2\times2$ matrix over $\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z$, viewed as an invertible matrix, belongs to the image of that $\rho$. The existence premise fails at any $x$ with $\mathcal B=\varnothing$.

For each finite place $v$ of $M$ of residue characteristic different from $2$, multiplicative reduction at one place of $F$ over $v$ implies multiplicative reduction at every place of $F$ over $v$. The set $\mathcal B$ of finite places of $F$ with multiplicative reduction is finite, each finite place has positive inertia degree over $\mathbb Q$, and for every natural number $q$ the sum of inertia degrees of the places in $\mathcal B$ of residue characteristic $q$ is at most $[F:\mathbb Q]$. The uniformizer is nonzero, with norm less than $1$, and the norms of nonzero elements of $F_w$ are its norm's integer powers. Write $m(w)$ for the natural-number order determined by $\|q_w\|_w=\|\pi_w\|_w^{m(w)}$, where $\|\cdot\|_w$ is the completion norm. Write $p(w)$ for the characteristic of the residue field of $w$.
