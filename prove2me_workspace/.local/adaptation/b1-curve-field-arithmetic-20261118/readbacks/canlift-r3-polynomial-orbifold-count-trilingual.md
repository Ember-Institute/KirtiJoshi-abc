# R3 polynomial orbifold count and Weierstrass injectivity

## English

Let $K$ be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Take a nonconstant polynomial $s\in K[X]$ and a set $E\subset K$ of three marked points. The multiplicity $m_E(w)$ is 2 on $E$ and 1 elsewhere. The ramification index $e_s(w)$ is the multiplicity of $w$ as a root of $s-s(w)$. `polynomial_orbifold_count` assumes that $e_s(w)m_E(w)$ is constant on each fiber of $s$. In Lean, if `s.eval w = s.eval w'`, then `polynomialRamificationIdx s w * hemiMultiplicity E w = polynomialRamificationIdx s w' * hemiMultiplicity E w'`. [Ramification and multiplicity](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftPolynomialDegree.lean#L33) · [Classification](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftPolynomialOrbifoldCount.lean#L350) · [Pinned R3 count](https://github.com/lana-agents/oka/blob/75dcdc3faadd10b986afb9283e89902bcdddecd4/Oka/Uniformization/R3Count.lean)

Write $D=\deg s$. For each image $v$ of a critical or marked point, let $M_v$ be the common value of $e_s(w)m_E(w)$. Let $n_v,a_v$ count the unmarked and marked preimages. Summing the ramification indices gives $2D=M_v(2n_v+a_v)$. The derivative gives $\sum_v(D-n_v-a_v)=D-1$, and $\sum_v a_v=3$. For $D\ge2$, these identities and the parity of $M_v$ on marked fibers give exactly two such values. One of their fibers has a single point $c$, so its fiber polynomial is a nonzero multiple of $(X-c)^D$. [Fiber identity](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftPolynomialOrbifoldCount.lean#L216) · [Derivative count](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftPolynomialOrbifoldCount.lean#L162) · [Two fibers](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftPolynomialOrbifoldCount.lean#L65) · [Singleton fiber](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftPolynomialOrbifoldCount.lean#L333)

- Degree 1 is the first alternative.
- In degree 2, the two fibers contain one and two marked points. Reflection about $c$ preserves $E$, so $2c-w\in E$ for every $w\in E$.
- In degree 3, the singleton is unmarked and all three marked points lie in the other fiber. There are $c,\rho\in K$ such that $(w-c)^3=\rho$ for every $w\in E$. [Degree alternatives](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftPolynomialOrbifoldCount.lean#L350)

For an elliptic Weierstrass curve $W$ over $\mathbb C$, `E₂ W` is the set of the three distinct roots of its 2-division cubic. Its polynomial is $4X^3+b_2X^2+2b_4X+b_6$. The degree-two reflection yields $c_6(W)=0$ and $j(W)=1728$. The degree-three relation yields $c_4(W)=0$ and $j(W)=0$. In each case the cubic and the symmetry give a polynomial of degree at most two that vanishes at all three roots. Under $j(W)\ne0$ and $j(W)\ne1728$, `polynomial_eval_injective_of_hemi_orbifold` obtains degree 1 from the classification and gives `Function.Injective s.eval`. [Two-division roots](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftPolynomialRigidity.lean#L30) · [Invariant calculation and injectivity](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftWeierstrassOrbifoldCount.lean#L45)

The analytic connection remains open. It must produce the polynomial on the first-coordinate line from the uniformization and the relevant commensurator action. It must also prove the fiber premise from the local ramification and orbifold multiplicities. With equality of the polynomial values at the two first coordinates, injectivity gives equality of the coordinates themselves. `canLift27Complex_of_uniformization` takes a uniformization with this fixed-coordinate property for each curve outside `canLiftExceptionalJ`.
