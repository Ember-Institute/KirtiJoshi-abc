### The lattice and its cover

Write $\mathbb H$ for the upper half-plane in $\mathbb C$. A matrix $g=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$ in $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ acts by $g\cdot z=(az+b)/(cz+d)$. Write $\langle A,B\rangle$ and $\langle A,B,-I_2\rangle$ for the subgroups generated by the listed matrices. Lean calls the latter group `IsUniformization.pmGroup A B`. [Generated subgroup](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftDeckGroup.lean:75)

Let $L=(\omega_1,\omega_2)$ be an ordered pair of complex numbers that are linearly independent over $\mathbb R$. Its period lattice is $\Lambda_L=\mathbb Z\omega_1+\mathbb Z\omega_2$. A given `U : LatticeComplementCover L` supplies a map $\Psi:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$. Its restriction $\psi$ to $\mathbb H$ takes values in $\mathbb C\setminus\Lambda_L$ and is a covering map onto this set. The data also require complex differentiability on $\mathbb H$ and a nonzero derivative at each point there. Write $G_U$ for `U.deckGroup`, the matrix group for the map $z\mapsto\psi(z)+\Lambda_L$.

$$
G_U=\left\{g\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)\,\middle|\,
\exists\,\lambda\in\Lambda_L\ \forall z\in\mathbb H,\quad
\psi(g\cdot z)=\psi(z)+\lambda\right\}.
$$

### `LatticeComplementCover.sub_mem_lattice_iff`

For any such $L$ and $U$ and any $z,w\in\mathbb H$, the two images represent the same class modulo $\Lambda_L$ exactly when an element of $G_U$ takes $z$ to $w$.

$$
\psi(w)-\psi(z)\in\Lambda_L
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad

### The lattice model and its coordinates

For $\tau\in\mathbb H$, take the ordered period pair $(\tau,1)$ and its lattice $\Lambda_\tau=\mathbb Z\tau+\mathbb Z$. Let $E_\tau$ denote the short Weierstrass model `latticeCurve τ` with lattice invariants $g_2=60\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda_\tau\setminus\{0\}}\lambda^{-4}$ and $g_3=140\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda_\tau\setminus\{0\}}\lambda^{-6}$.

$$
E_\tau\colon Y^2=X^3-\frac{g_2(\Lambda_\tau)}{4}X-\frac{g_3(\Lambda_\tau)}{4}.
$$

For a Weierstrass change of variables $C=(u,r,s,t)$, where $u\in\mathbb C^\times$ and $r,s,t\in\mathbb C$, write $C\cdot E_\tau$ for the resulting equation. In that equation's coordinates, the map $P_{\tau,C}=\texttt{latticeParameterization}\ \tau\ C$ uses the Weierstrass function $\wp_\tau$ of $\Lambda_\tau$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
X(z)&=\wp_\tau(z), & Y(z)&=\wp'_\tau(z)/2,\\
P_{\tau,C}(z)&=
\left(\frac{X(z)-r}{u^2},\ \frac{Y(z)-t-s\bigl(X(z)-r\bigr)}{u^3}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

### `isUniformization_latticeParameterization`

Fix $\tau\in\mathbb H$, a change of variables $C$, matrices $A,B\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$, and a map $\psi:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$. Assume all of the following.

- For every $z\in\mathbb H$, $\psi(z)\notin\Lambda_\tau$.
- The map $\psi$ is complex differentiable at every $z\in\mathbb H$ and its derivative there is nonzero.
- For every $v\in\mathbb C\setminus\Lambda_\tau$, there is a $z\in\mathbb H$ with $\psi(z)=v$.
- For every $z,w\in\mathbb H$, the displayed equivalence holds.

$$
\psi(w)-\psi(z)\in\Lambda_\tau
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\,\gamma\in\langle A,B,-I_2\rangle,\quad \gamma\cdot z=w.
$$

Then $f=P_{\tau,C}\circ\psi:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C^2$ is an `IsUniformization` of $C\cdot E_\tau$ with $A,B$. It is complex differentiable with nonzero derivative at each $z\in\mathbb H$. Its image of $\mathbb H$ is the set of all affine points of $C\cdot E_\tau$. For every $z,w\in\mathbb H$, its fibers satisfy

$$
f(z)=f(w)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\,\gamma\in\langle A,B\rangle,\quad \gamma\cdot z=w.
$$

The scalar matrix $-I_2$ acts as the identity on $\mathbb H$. Each element of $\langle A,B,-I_2\rangle$ therefore acts as an element of $\langle A,B\rangle$. Its conclusion is the uniformization by $P_{\tau,C}\circ U.\Psi$ with the matrices $A,B$. For this $U$, `Generators.A U` and `Generators.B U` are the matrices chosen by the edge pairings. $$

\psi(B\cdot z)=\psi(z)+\tau,\qquad G_U=\langle A,B,-I_2\rangle.

$\tau\in\mathbb H$、変数変換 $C$、行列 $A,B\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$、写像 $\psi:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ を固定し、次の条件を仮定する。

- 各 $z\in\mathbb H$ に対して $\psi(z)\notin\Lambda_\tau$ が成り立つ。
- $\psi$ は $\mathbb H$ の各点で複素微分可能であり、その導関数の値は零でない。
- 各 $v\in\mathbb C\setminus\Lambda_\tau$ に対して、$\psi(z)=v$ を満たす $z\in\mathbb H$ が存在する。
- 任意の $z,w\in\mathbb H$ について、次に示す同値関係が成り立つ。

$$
\psi(w)-\psi(z)\in\Lambda_\tau
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\,\gamma\in\langle A,B,-I_2\rangle,\quad \gamma\cdot z=w.
$$

このとき、合成写像 $f=P_{\tau,C}\circ\psi:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C^2$ は $A,B$ による $C\cdot E_\tau$ の一様化を与える。$f$ は $\mathbb H$ の各点で複素微分可能であり、その導関数の値は零でない。$f$ による $\mathbb H$ の像は $C\cdot E_\tau$ のアフィン点全体である。任意の $z,w\in\mathbb H$ について、ファイバーは次の同値関係を満たす。

$$
f(z)=f(w)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\,\gamma\in\langle A,B\rangle,\quad \gamma\cdot z=w.
$$

スカラー行列 $-I_2$ は $\mathbb H$ 上で恒等変換として作用する。このため、$\langle A,B,-I_2\rangle$ の各元は $\langle A,B\rangle$ のある元と同じ作用を持つ。 [形式的な命題](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftLatticeParameterization.lean:160) · [生成された部分群の作用](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftLatticeParameterization.lean:136) · [一様化の定義](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftUniformizationCover.lean:69)
