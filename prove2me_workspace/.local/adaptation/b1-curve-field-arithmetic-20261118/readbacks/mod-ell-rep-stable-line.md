## `hasCyclicSubgroup_of_stable_line`

### English

Let $F$ be a number field, let $\overline F$ be a chosen algebraic closure of $F$, and let $E$ be an elliptic Weierstrass curve over $F$. Write $E(\overline F)$ for its group of nonsingular affine points over $\overline F$ together with the identity at infinity $O$. The automorphisms of $\overline F$ fixing $F$ act on the affine coordinates and fix $O$. Take a natural number $\ell$ with a proof that it is prime, allowing $\ell=2$, and a mod-$\ell$ representation record $R$ for these data. It consists of an additive isomorphism $\iota$ from the subgroup of $E(\overline F)$ killed by $\ell$ to $(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^2$, and a group homomorphism $\rho$ from the automorphisms of $\overline F$ fixing $F$ into $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)$ with open kernel. For each such automorphism $\sigma$ and each point $Q$ killed by $\ell$, the data satisfy $\iota(\sigma Q)=\rho(\sigma)\iota(Q)$. Let $v$ be any nonzero column vector in $(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^2$. Suppose that for every automorphism $\sigma$ of $\overline F$ fixing $F$ there exists a scalar $a\in\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z$ with $\rho(\sigma)v=av$. Then there exists an additive subgroup $H$ of $E(\overline F)$ with exactly $\ell$ elements such that every automorphism of $\overline F$ fixing $F$ sends every point of $H$ into $H$.
