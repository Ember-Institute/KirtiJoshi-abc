# The model covering square and distinguished cusp

Upstream / 上游 / 上流 `lana-agents/iut@07cb1da58f6d428a670dbb5dd6093d428bcbcdd8`

## English

Let $F$ be a number field, $E/F$ a Weierstrass elliptic curve, and $\bar F$ an algebraic closure of $F$. Let `VBad` be the specified set of finite places of the field of moduli. For `P : AdmissiblePrimeData F E Fbar VBad`, let $\ell=P.\ell$ and $K=P.\mathrm{torsionField}$. `AdmissiblePrimeData.toJoshi` carries the prime, the torsion basis, and the representation to the Scene1 interface. It retains the fixed field $K$ of the representation kernel, the Tate parameters, and the local uniformizers. The equal norms give equal valuation orders for the Tate parameters. This transfers the condition that the orders are coprime to $\ell$. [Lean conversion](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutOrbicurveData.lean#L33)

Take `Pi1 : EtalePi1Theory.{u}`, a number-field instance for $K$, and `hcore : HasCoreUniversally (modelAG Pi1) F E`. The hypothesis `hcore` gives the sign quotient as the core after every characteristic-zero field extension. The definition `orbicurveData P Pi1 hcore` uses $M\subseteq E(K)[\ell]$, the order-$\ell$ line from the first torsion basis vector. The two level-$\ell$ orbicurves $\underline{X}_K$ and $\underline{C}_K$ have this line and sign flags `false` and `true`, respectively. The full $\ell$-torsion has order $\ell^2$, giving types $(1,\ell\text{-tors})$ and $(1,\ell\text{-tors})^\pm$. The level-one models $X_K$ and $C_K$ are the base changes of the once-punctured curve and its sign quotient. The horizontal covers have multiplier $\ell$. The vertical sign quotient covers have multiplier $1$. These four covers satisfy the model's cartesian square predicate. [Lean models and covers](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutOrbicurveData.lean#L75) · [Cartesian predicate](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ReferenceIut/Iut/Anabelian/Model.lean#L261) · [Upstream models](../../../../../refer/iut/Iut/Anabelian/Existence.lean#L217)

The proof specializes `hcore` to $K$ to obtain $C_K$ as the core of $X_K$. It uses core invariance under covers along $X_K\to C_K$ and $\underline{C}_K\to C_K$ to get the same core for $\underline{C}_K$. The second basis coordinate identifies $Q=E(K)[\ell]/M$ with $\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z$. The class $q=[e_2]$ maps to $1$, so it is nonzero. The cusp $\varepsilon$ is the image of $q$ under `cuspOf` on $\underline{C}_K$. 证明将 `hcore` 特化到 $K$，得到 $C_K$ 为 $X_K$ 的核心。沿 $X_K\to C_K$ 和 $\underline{C}_K\to C_K$ 应用核心在覆盖下的不变性，得到 $\underline{C}_K$ 的核心同样为 $C_K$。扭点基的第二坐标将 $Q=E(K)[\ell]/M$ 识别为 $\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z$。类 $q=[e_2]$ 映至 $1$，故非零。特选尖点 $\varepsilon$ 是 $\underline{C}_K$ 上 `cuspOf` 对 $q$ 取的像。 [Lean 构造](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutOrbicurveData.lean#L147) · [Scene1 商](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/InitialThetaTorsionQuotient.lean#L71) · [上游商及构造](../../../../../refer/iut/Iut/Anabelian/Existence.lean#L310)

## 日本語

`Pi1 : EtalePi1Theory.{u}`、$K$ の数体インスタンス、および `hcore : HasCoreUniversally (modelAG Pi1) F E` を取る。仮定 `hcore` により、標数零の任意の体拡大への底変換後も符号商がコアとなる。`orbicurveData P Pi1 hcore` は、第 1 基底ベクトルが生成する位数 $\ell$ の直線 $M\subseteq E(K)[\ell]$ を用いる。レベル $\ell$ の軌道曲線 $\underline{X}_K$ と $\underline{C}_K$ は同じ直線を持ち、符号フラグはそれぞれ `false` と `true` である。$\ell$ ねじれ点全体の位数は $\ell^2$ なので、二つの型はそれぞれ $(1,\ell\text{-tors})$ と $(1,\ell\text{-tors})^\pm$ である。レベル一のモデル $X_K$ と $C_K$ は、一点を除いた曲線とその符号商をそれぞれ底変換して得る。横向きの被覆の乗数は $\ell$ で、縦向きの符号商の被覆の乗数は $1$ である。四つの被覆はモデルのカルテジアン図式の述語を満たす。 [Lean のモデルと被覆](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutOrbicurveData.lean#L75) · [カルテジアン図式の述語](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ReferenceIut/Iut/Anabelian/Model.lean#L261) · [上流のモデル](../../../../../refer/iut/Iut/Anabelian/Existence.lean#L217)
