## English

Fix a tower of fields $k\to K\to\Omega$ and $t\in\Omega$. Use $k(t)\subseteq K(t)$ for the intermediate fields generated by $t$ in $\Omega$. Assume for `exists_galois_constantExtension` that $\Omega$ is algebraically closed and $k$ has characteristic zero. Given a finite intermediate extension $F/k(t)$, the theorem gives a finite Galois extension $N/k(t)$ containing $F$ and proves that the compositum $N'=K(t)(N)$ in $\Omega$ is finite Galois over $K(t)$. Take a primitive element of $F$ and its minimal polynomial $p$ over $k(t)$. The coefficient map from $k(t)$ to $K(t)$ preserves the root set in $\Omega$, since both polynomials have the same coefficients after mapping to $\Omega$. Adjoin this common root set to each base field to obtain the two splitting fields. Characteristic zero gives separability. [Root set](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftConstantExtensionGalois.lean#L31) · [Adapted Galois closure](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftConstantExtensionGalois.lean#L40) · [Pi1 Galois closure](https://github.com/lana-agents/pi1/blob/ca7def953193f861a462aa0831eec8f0a31ff69f/Pi1/Orbicurve/BaseChange.lean#L734)

For normal $N/k(t)$ and any $N'/K(t)$ containing $N$ inside $\Omega$, `constantExtensionRes` restricts a $K(t)$-automorphism $\sigma'$ of $N'$ to a $k(t)$-automorphism of $N$. For $x\in N$, its minimal polynomial over $k(t)$ still vanishes at $\sigma'(x)$, since $\sigma'$ fixes the coefficients. Normality puts all the roots in $N$. Applying the same argument to the inverse shows that the restriction is an automorphism. When $N'=K(t)(N)$, restriction is injective because a $K(t)$-automorphism fixing $N$ fixes every generator of $N'$. [Restriction](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftConstantExtensionGalois.lean#L152) · [Injectivity](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftConstantExtensionGalois.lean#L210) · [Pi1 restriction](https://github.com/lana-agents/pi1/blob/ca7def953193f861a462aa0831eec8f0a31ff69f/Pi1/Orbicurve/BaseChange.lean#L334)

If $F\subseteq N$ and $F'=K(t)(F)$ inside $\Omega$, then $\sigma'$ fixes $F'$ pointwise if and only if its restriction fixes $F$ pointwise. For the implication from $F$ to $F'$, $\sigma'$ already fixes $K(t)$, so fixing $F$ fixes the elements generated over $K(t)$. [Fixing subgroups](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftConstantExtensionGalois.lean#L223)

Write $A$ and $A'$ for the integral closures of $k[t]$ in $N$ and $K[t]$ in $N'$. The normalization-ring map $\phi\colon A\to A'$ commutes with the action of $\sigma'$ on $A'$ and of its restriction $\sigma$ on $A$. For any ideal $P'\subseteq A'$, if $\sigma'$ is in the inertia group of $P'$, then $\sigma$ is in the inertia group of $\phi^{-1}(P')$. Indeed, for $a\in A$, the image of $\sigma(a)-a$ under $\phi$ is $\sigma'(\phi(a))-\phi(a)\in P'$. [Equivariance](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftConstantExtensionGalois.lean#L240) · [Inertia](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftConstantExtensionGalois.lean#L249) · [Pi1 inertia](https://github.com/lana-agents/pi1/blob/ca7def953193f861a462aa0831eec8f0a31ff69f/Pi1/Orbicurve/BaseChange.lean#L415)

For `constantExtensionRes_surjective`, assume $k$ is algebraically closed, $t$ is transcendental over $K$, and $N/k(t)$ and $N'/K(t)$ are finite Galois with $N\subseteq N'$ in $\Omega$. An element of $N$ fixed by the image of restriction is fixed in $N'$ by every $K(t)$-automorphism of $N'$, so it belongs to $K(t)$. It is algebraic over $k(t)$ because it lies in $N$. Relative algebraic closedness gives $N\cap K(t)=k(t)$. The image of restriction thus has fixed field $k(t)$ in $N$. The finite Galois correspondence makes the image the whole group.
