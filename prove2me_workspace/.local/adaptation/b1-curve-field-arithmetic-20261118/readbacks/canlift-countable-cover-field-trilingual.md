# A countable field for the curve and two cover coordinates

## English

Let $K$ be algebraically closed of characteristic zero, let $\Omega$ be an extension field, and take $t\in\Omega$. Write $K(t)$ for `rationalBase K t`, the intermediate field generated by $t$ in $\Omega$. Let $E/K$ be an elliptic Weierstrass curve and $L/K(t)$ a finite intermediate extension in $\Omega$. Fix the two elements $s,y\in L$. [Base field](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/FunctionFieldNormalization.lean#L51) · [Theorem](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftCountableCoverField.lean#L93)

The theorem `exists_countable_cover_field` gives a countable algebraically closed subfield $k\subseteq K$ and an elliptic Weierstrass curve $W/k$ whose coefficientwise base change to $K$ equals $E$. It gives a finite intermediate extension $F/k(t)$ in the same $\Omega$. Both $s$ and $y$, viewed as elements of $\Omega$, lie in $F$. The conclusions $F\subseteq L$ and $L\subseteq K(t)(F)$ imply

$$
K(t)(F)=L
$$

as subfields of $\Omega$, since $L$ contains $K(t)$ and $F$. Here $K(t)(F)$ is the field generated over $K(t)$ by the actual elements of $F$. [Inclusions and coordinates](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftCountableCoverField.lean#L97)

The proof chooses a primitive element $\theta$ of the finite separable extension $L/K(t)$. It writes $s$ and $y$ as polynomials in $\theta$ over $K(t)$. Take the coefficients of those polynomials and of the monic minimal polynomial of $\theta$. Express these coefficients as rational functions of $t$ over $K$. Adjoin their numerator and denominator coefficients and the five Weierstrass coefficients of $E$ to the prime field. The algebraic closure of this finitely generated field inside $K$ gives $k$. A field generated by finitely many elements over the prime field is countable. Its algebraic closure remains countable. The monic polynomial descends to $k(t)$ and proves that $F=k(t)(\theta)$ is finite over $k(t)$. Evaluating the two coordinate polynomials gives membership of $s$ and $y$ in this same $F$. ## 中文

设 $K$ 为特征零的代数闭域，$\Omega$ 为其扩域，并取 $t\in\Omega$。用 $K(t)$ 表示 `rationalBase K t`，即在 $\Omega$ 中由 $t$ 生成的中间域。设 $E/K$ 为椭圆 Weierstrass 曲线，$L/K(t)$ 为 $\Omega$ 内的有限中间扩张。固定两个元素 $s,y\in L$。 [底域](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/FunctionFieldNormalization.lean#L51) · [定理](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftCountableCoverField.lean#L93)

`exists_countable_cover_field` 给出可数代数闭子域 $k\subseteq K$ 和椭圆 Weierstrass 曲线 $W/k$。将 $W$ 的系数扩到 $K$ 得到 $E$。定理还给出同一 $\Omega$ 内的有限中间扩张 $F/k(t)$，其中 $s,y$ 作为 $\Omega$ 中的实际元素均属于 $F$。定理给出 $F\subseteq L$ 与 $L\subseteq K(t)(F)$。由于 $L$ 包含 $K(t)$ 和 $F$，在 $\Omega$ 中有

$$
K(t)(F)=L.
$$

这里 $K(t)(F)$ 为由 $F$ 的实际元素在 $K(t)$ 上生成的域。 [包含关系与两个坐标](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftCountableCoverField.lean#L97)

证明先为有限可分扩张 $L/K(t)$ 选本原元 $\theta$，把 $s,y$ 写成 $\theta$ 上系数属于 $K(t)$ 的多项式。取这两个多项式及 $\theta$ 的首一最小多项式的系数，将它们写成 $t$ 的有理函数。把分子和分母的系数及 $E$ 的五个 Weierstrass 系数加入素域，并取所得域在 $K$ 中的代数闭包，得到 $k$。素域上的有限生成域及其代数闭包均可数。首一多项式下降到 $k(t)$，证明 $F=k(t)(\theta)$ 在 $k(t)$ 上有限。对两个坐标多项式求值，得到 $s,y$ 同属此 $F$。 [系数模型](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftCountableCoverField.lean#L47) · [本原元与模型](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftCountableCoverField.lean#L107) · [Pi1 参考](https://github.com/lana-agents/pi1/blob/ca7def953193f861a462aa0831eec8f0a31ff69f/Pi1/Orbicurve/LefschetzMain.lean#L129)

`exists_countable_cover_field` は可算な代数閉部分体 $k\subseteq K$ と、楕円 Weierstrass 曲線 $W/k$ を与える。$W$ の係数を $K$ に移すと $E$ になる。同じ $\Omega$ 内の有限拡大 $F/k(t)$ も得られ、$\Omega$ の元とみなした $s,y$ はともに $F$ に属する。定理が与える包含関係は $F\subseteq L$ と $L\subseteq K(t)(F)$ である。$L$ は $K(t)$ と $F$ を含むので、$\Omega$ の部分体として

$$
K(t)(F)=L
$$

が成り立つ。ここで $K(t)(F)$ は $F$ の元を $K(t)$ に添加して得る体である。 [包含関係と二つの座標](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftCountableCoverField.lean#L97)
