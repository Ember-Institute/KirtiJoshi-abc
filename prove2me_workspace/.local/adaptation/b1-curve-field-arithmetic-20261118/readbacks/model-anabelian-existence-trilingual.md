# Anabelian existence on the orbicurve model

Upstream / 上游 / 上流 `lana-agents/iut@07cb1da58f6d428a670dbb5dd6093d428bcbcdd8`

## English

For `Pi1 : EtalePi1Theory.{u}` and `T : TemperedPi1Theory Pi1`, `anabelianExistence Pi1 T` proves `AnabelianExistence (modelAG Pi1) (modelTG Pi1 T)`. Its field `exists_data` takes a number field $F$, an elliptic curve $E/F$, and an algebraic closure $\bar F$. The field also takes the set of bad places `VBad` of the field of moduli. It takes admissible prime data `P` with torsion field $K=P.\mathrm{torsionField}$, a number field. It takes a Tate family `TF` on $K$ at the prime of `P`. The interface assumes `IsInitialThetaGlobalData F E Fbar VBad` and `HasCoreUniversally (modelAG Pi1) F E`. It uses the canonical inclusion of the field of moduli $F_{\mathrm{mod}}$ into $K$. The result gives `O : OrbicurveData` and proves `Nonempty` for the `LocalThetaData` type indexed by that same `O`. [Lean interface](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutInitialThetaData.lean#L26) · [Lean theorem](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutAnabelianExistence.lean#L112) · [Upstream theorem](../../../../../refer/iut/Iut/Anabelian/Existence.lean#L574)

The proof takes `O := orbicurveData P Pi1 hcore`. It uses the Scene1 place section for the transported prime data and Tate family. The section chooses a place of $K$ over each finite or infinite place of $F_{\mathrm{mod}}$. The four covers still meet the model's cartesian square condition after base change to each selected finite completion. At each finite place of $K$, the chosen decomposition group is the stabilizer of a prime above that place. The group is closed in the Krull topology. [Construction](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutAnabelianExistence.lean#L125) · [Scene1 place section](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/InitialThetaValuationSection.lean#L323) · [Decomposition group](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ReferenceIut/Iut/Cor312/ThetaData/PlacesOver.lean#L174) · [Closedness](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ReferenceIut/Iut/Cor312/ThetaData/PlacesOver.lean#L224) · [Upstream local data](../../../../../refer/iut/Iut/Anabelian/Existence.lean#L509)

Above a place in `VBad`, the image of the global line $M$ is the graph line of the selected Tate structure. Injectivity of the point map preserves its order $\ell$. The construction uses the very same structure from `TF` on both local orbicurves. The graph-line fact gives both the required local type and the theta-root predicate on the local $\underline{X}_K$. [Graph line and order](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutAnabelianExistence.lean#L57) · [Local type and theta-root predicate](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutAnabelianExistence.lean#L128) · [Upstream local type](../../../../../refer/iut/Iut/Anabelian/Existence.lean#L492)

The image of the second torsion basis vector $e_2$ supplies a canonical generator at the selected bad place. Every canonical local generator lifts to global $\ell$-torsion and has quotient class $[e_2]$ or $-[e_2]$. These classes give the same cusp on the signed model $\underline{C}_K$. Thus the base change of the distinguished cusp is the value of `canonicalGraphCusp` at the chosen Tate structure. 証明では `O := orbicurveData P Pi1 hcore` とおく。移し替えた素数データと Tate 族に Scene1 の素点の切断を適用する。この切断は、$F_{\mathrm{mod}}$ の各有限素点または無限素点の上にある $K$ の素点を一つ選ぶ。四つの被覆は、選んだ各有限素点での完備化に底変換しても、モデルのカルテジアン図式の条件を満たす。$K$ の各有限素点に対して選ぶ分解群は、その上の素イデアルの安定化群である。この群は Krull 位相で閉である。 [構成](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutAnabelianExistence.lean#L125) · [Scene1 の素点の切断](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/InitialThetaValuationSection.lean#L323) · [分解群](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ReferenceIut/Iut/Cor312/ThetaData/PlacesOver.lean#L174) · [閉性](../../../../../ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ReferenceIut/Iut/Cor312/ThetaData/PlacesOver.lean#L224) · [上流の局所データ](../../../../../refer/iut/Iut/Anabelian/Existence.lean#L509)
