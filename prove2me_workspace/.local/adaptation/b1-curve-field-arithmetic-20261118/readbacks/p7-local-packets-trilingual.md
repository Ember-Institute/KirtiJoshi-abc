# P7 · Local order and tensor packets

## English

- At a finite place $w$ of residue characteristic $p$, `norm_le_one_of_ordp_nonneg` gives $\lVert x\rVert\le 1$ whenever $\operatorname{ord}_p(x)\ge 0$, including $x=0$. For $x\ne 0$ and $m\in\mathbb N$, `ordp_div_residueChar_pow` gives $\operatorname{ord}_p(x/p^m)=\operatorname{ord}_p(x)-m$. [Unit ball](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutConcreteLocalOrder.lean:23) · [Division by a prime power](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutConcreteLocalOrder.lean:36)
- At a rational prime $p$, `IsOver K p v` requires a finite place $v$ with residue characteristic $p$. In this case the factor is the quotient of the completion by the zero ideal, and its $\mathbb Q_p$-algebra map is `padicEmb` followed by `factorMk`. A finite place of different residue characteristic or an infinite place gives the quotient by the whole ring, hence the zero ring with its unique $\mathbb Q_p$-algebra map. Given `c j = Place.finite w`, `incl` transports $K_w$ along the place equality, takes the quotient, and inserts the result into the $j$-th slot with $1$ in the other slots. - At the rational infinite place, the scalar field is $\mathbb R$. `ArchFactor` uses the real or complex model according to the infinite place of $K$. These are the bottom and top $\mathbb R$-subalgebras of $\mathbb C$ respectively. For a finite place of $K$ supplied in this branch, `archPlace` selects an arbitrary infinite place of $K$. [Infinite-place selection](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutConcreteLocalPacket.lean:209) · [Real and complex models](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/ArithmeticTeichmullerSpaces/Act1Scene1/LocalArchimedeanTensor.lean:49) · [Scalar field](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutConcreteLocalPacket.lean:235)
- `Tensor` is the indexed tensor product of the factors. The sup norm in the basis of elementary tensors gives the finite-dimensional module topology. The tensor is a second-countable topological ring and a proper metric space. Its metric is ultrametric at $p$. [Tensor and basis](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutConcreteLocalPacket.lean:283) · [Topology](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutConcreteLocalPacket.lean:306) · [Ultrametricity](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutConcreteLocalPacket.lean:371)
- If a factor at $p$ is a zero ring, its dimension is zero, so `PacketIndex` is empty and the coordinate model gives a zero ring for the entire tensor. For an empty index type $\iota$, there is one basis index. Mathlib’s `PiTensorProduct.isEmptyEquiv` sends the empty pure tensor to $1$. Its inverse is the scalar map $r\mapsto r\cdot 1$, giving the scalar algebra $\mathbb Q_p$ at $p$ or $\mathbb R$ at infinity. [Zero factor](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutConcreteLocalPacket.lean:135) · [Basis indices](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/abc/prove2me_workspace/Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutConcreteLocalPacket.lean:291) · [Empty tensor](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/mathlib4-0df444a/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/PiTensorProduct/Basic.lean:779) · [Tensor unit](/home/admin-wsl/Coq/mathlib4-0df444a/Mathlib/RingTheory/PiTensorProduct.lean:37)
- Under `hc : ∀ j, IsOver K p (c j)`, `genuinePacket` uses the completions themselves. The condition holds vacuously for an empty index type. The factorwise quotient maps induce the $\mathbb Q_p$-algebra equivalence `genuineTensorEquiv`. The forward map applies `factorMk` to each component of a pure tensor, and the inverse applies the inverse factor equivalences. `genuineTensorContinuousLinearEquiv` makes the algebra equivalence a homeomorphism for the two coordinate norms. The two norm estimates use the operator norms of the map and its inverse.
