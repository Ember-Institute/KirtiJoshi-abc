## BoundaryKernelProducts

设 $k$ 是闭球紧致的非平凡赋范域，$B=\mathbb P^1(\mathbb R)$，$Y=\mathbb P^1(k)$。两条射影直线均取一点紧化上的博雷尔结构。`polishSpace_onePoint` 断言 $Y$ 的拓扑第二可数且可由完备度量给出。`D3set` 是 $Y^3$ 中坐标两两互异的三元组集合。该集合可测，且任意 $g\in\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$ 的逐坐标作用下，它的逆像等于自身。马尔可夫核 $\kappa$ 从 $B$ 取值于 $Y$ 上的概率测度。对每个 $b\in B$，`kern2` 在 $b$ 处给出 $\kappa(b)\otimes\kappa(b)$，`kern3` 给出 $\kappa(b)\otimes(\kappa(b)\otimes\kappa(b))$。对带有可判定相等性的 $k$，若 $b,b'\in B$ 满足 $\kappa(b')=g_*\kappa(b)$，`kern_map` 给出两种乘积核在 $b'$ 处各自等于在 $b$ 处测度的逐坐标推前。`TripleNull` 使用三重积与互异三元组集合。`FromProb` 由三重积在互异三元组上为零推出测度集中于至多两点，再用二重积的对角线质量处理原子。

Let $k$ be a nontrivially normed field whose closed balls are compact. Put $B=\mathbb P^1(\mathbb R)$ and $Y=\mathbb P^1(k)$, each with the Borel structure of the one-point compactification. By `polishSpace_onePoint`, $Y$ is second countable and has a compatible complete metric. The measurable set `D3set` consists of triples in $Y^3$ with pairwise distinct coordinates. Its preimage under the coordinatewise action of any $g\in\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$ equals itself.

The Markov kernel $\kappa$ assigns each $b\in B$ a probability measure on $Y$. At $b$, `kern2` gives $\kappa(b)\otimes\kappa(b)$ and `kern3` gives $\kappa(b)\otimes(\kappa(b)\otimes\kappa(b))$. Equip $k$ with decidable equality. If $\kappa(b')=g_*\kappa(b)$, then `kern_map` gives the corresponding coordinatewise pushforward identity for each product kernel. `TripleNull` uses the triple product and the set of distinct triples. When the triple product gives zero mass to distinct triples, `FromProb` shows that the measure is carried by at most two points. It uses the diagonal mass of the double product to handle atoms.

## TripleNull

继续用 $B,Y$ 和 $\kappa$，令 $\mathrm{bdry}$ 为实直线的勒贝格测度沿 $\mathbb R\hookrightarrow B$ 的推前。设 $\Gamma\le\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ 可数，$a:\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)\to\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$，并取 $k$ 上的可判定相等性。双遍历性要求每个在 $\Gamma$ 对角作用下不变的可测集合 $E\subseteq B^2$，其自身或其补集在 $\mathrm{bdry}\otimes\mathrm{bdry}$ 下为零测集。`Unbounded Γ a` 指存在序列 $\gamma_n\in\Gamma$，使 $a(\gamma_n)(0,1,\infty)$ 收敛到有重复坐标的三元组。若上述双遍历性与 `Unbounded Γ a` 成立，且对每个 $\gamma\in\Gamma$ 都有 $\kappa(\gamma b)=a(\gamma)_*\kappa(b)$ 对 $\mathrm{bdry}$ 几乎处处的 $b$ 成立，则 `triple_null_of_unbounded` 给出 $\kappa(b)^{\otimes3}(\mathrm{D3set})=0$ 对 $\mathrm{bdry}$ 几乎处处的 $b$ 成立。证明将三重积限制到 `D3set`，得到有限核 `mk3`。`mk3pair` 在边界点对 $(b_1,b_2)$ 处取这两个受限测度的乘积。`exists_exhaustion` 给出递增开集 $C_n$ 和紧集 $K_n$，满足 $C_n\subseteq K_n\subseteq\mathrm{D3set}$ 且 $\bigcup_n C_n=\mathrm{D3set}$。上游 `commensurator_superrigidity` 先取 `Furstenberg` 构造的边界核，再把这个零测结论传给 `FromProb` 的 `exists_pointMap_of_triple_null`。

Keep $B$, $Y$, and $\kappa$ as above. Let $\mathrm{bdry}$ be the pushforward of Lebesgue measure along $\mathbb R\hookrightarrow B$. Let $\Gamma\le\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ be countable and let $a:\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)\to\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$, with decidable equality on $k$. Consider any measurable $E\subseteq B^2$ invariant under the diagonal $\Gamma$-action. In double ergodicity, either $E$ or its complement has $\mathrm{bdry}\otimes\mathrm{bdry}$ measure zero. The predicate `Unbounded Γ a` means that there is a sequence $\gamma_n\in\Gamma$ for which $a(\gamma_n)(0,1,\infty)$ converges to a triple with a repeated coordinate.

Assume double ergodicity and `Unbounded Γ a`. For each $\gamma\in\Gamma$, assume $\kappa(\gamma b)=a(\gamma)_*\kappa(b)$ for $\mathrm{bdry}$-almost every $b$. Then `triple_null_of_unbounded` gives $\kappa(b)^{\otimes3}(\mathrm{D3set})=0$ for $\mathrm{bdry}$-almost every $b$. The proof restricts the triple product to `D3set` to obtain the finite kernel `mk3`. At a boundary pair $(b_1,b_2)$, `mk3pair` gives the product of the two restricted measures. By `exists_exhaustion`, there are increasing open sets $C_n$ and compact sets $K_n$ with $C_n\subseteq K_n\subseteq\mathrm{D3set}$ and $\bigcup_n C_n=\mathrm{D3set}$. The upstream theorem `commensurator_superrigidity` takes the boundary kernel from `Furstenberg`. It passes the zero-mass result to `exists_pointMap_of_triple_null` in `FromProb`.
