They satisfy $\mu_1+\mu_2=t$, $\mu_1\mu_2=d$, and $\lVert\mu_2\rVert<\lVert\mu_1\rVert$. The theorem `Padic.exists_degenerate_limit` applies the root result to the trace and determinant of $R\in\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb Q_p)$. Equip $\mathbb Q_p$ with decidable equality. If $\lVert\det R\rVert<\lVert\operatorname{tr}R\rVert^2$, then $R^n(0,1,\infty)$ converges in $\mathbb P^1(\mathbb Q_p)^3$ to a triple with at least two equal coordinates.
