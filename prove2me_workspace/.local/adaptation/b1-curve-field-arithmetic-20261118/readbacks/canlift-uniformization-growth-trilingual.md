## `polyBdd_fst_smul_of_commensurator`

### English

Let $W$ be a complex Weierstrass curve with nonzero discriminant, let $A,B\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$, and let $\pi\colon\mathbb C\to\mathbb C^2$. Write $\mathbb H$ for the upper half-plane, $\Gamma$ for the subgroup generated by $A$ and $B$, and $g\cdot z$ for the fractional linear action on $\mathbb H$. Assume that $\pi$ is complex differentiable with nonzero derivative at each point of $\mathbb H$, that its image on $\mathbb H$ is exactly the set of complex affine solutions of the Weierstrass equation of $W$, and that for every $z,w\in\mathbb H$, $\pi(z)=\pi(w)$ if and only if $\gamma\cdot z=w$ for some $\gamma\in\Gamma$. Let $F(z)$ be the first coordinate of $\pi(z)$. For every $n\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ such that $\Gamma\cap n\Gamma n^{-1}$ has finite index in both $\Gamma$ and $n\Gamma n^{-1}$, there exist $K\in\mathbb R$ and $d\in\mathbb N$ such that $\lvert F(n\cdot z)\rvert\le K(2+\lvert F(z)\rvert)^d$ for every $z\in\mathbb H$. The constants may depend on $n$, and $d=0$ is allowed.

## `exists_invariant_polynomial_generator_of_finite_commensurator_index`

### English

Let $W$ be a complex Weierstrass curve with nonzero discriminant, let $A,B\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$, and let $\pi\colon\mathbb C\to\mathbb C^2$. Write $\mathbb H$ for the upper half-plane, $\Gamma$ for the subgroup generated by $A$ and $B$, and $g\cdot z$ for the fractional linear action on $\mathbb H$. Assume that $\pi$ is complex differentiable with nonzero derivative at each point of $\mathbb H$, that its image on $\mathbb H$ is exactly the set of complex affine solutions of the Weierstrass equation of $W$, and that for every $z,w\in\mathbb H$, $\pi(z)=\pi(w)$ if and only if $\gamma\cdot z=w$ for some $\gamma\in\Gamma$. Let $F(z)$ be the first coordinate of $\pi(z)$. Let $N$ be the subgroup of all $g\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ for which $\Gamma\cap g\Gamma g^{-1}$ has finite index in both $\Gamma$ and $g\Gamma g^{-1}$. Suppose $\Gamma\cap N$ has finite index in $N$. Then there exists a polynomial $s\in\mathbb C[X]$ of positive degree such that $s(F(n\cdot z))=s(F(z))$ for every $n\in N$ and every $z\in\mathbb H$. For the same $s$ and any $z,w\in\mathbb H$, if $s(F(z))=s(F(w))$, then $n\cdot z=w$ for some $n\in N$.
