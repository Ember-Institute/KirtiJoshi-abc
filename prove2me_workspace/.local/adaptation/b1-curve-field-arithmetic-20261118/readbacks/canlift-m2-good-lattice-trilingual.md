# CanLift M2 Fricke lattice

$$
\mathrm{nA}=\begin{pmatrix}x-y/z & x/z^2 \\ x & y/z\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\mathrm{nB}=\begin{pmatrix}y-x/z & -y/z^2 \\ -y & x/z\end{pmatrix}.
$$

取正实数 $x,y,z$，使 $x^2+y^2+z^2=xyz$。令 $\Gamma$ 为正规形矩阵 `nA`、`nB` 在 $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ 中生成的子群。`discreteTopology_normal` 断言 $\Gamma$ 的诱导拓扑离散。若再有 $x<y+z$、$y<x+z$、$z<x+y$，`exists_strip_cover_normal` 给出一个 $\eta>0$，对每个上半平面点 $w$ 都存在 $\gamma\in\Gamma$，使 $-1\le\Re(\gamma w)\le1$ 且 $\Im(\gamma w)\ge\eta$。在这些三角不等式下，`goodLattice_normal` 将离散性与条带覆盖合并为 `GoodLattice Γ`。该性质还要求存在双曲面积有限的可测集 $F$，每个轨道都与 $F$ 相交，且对每个 $w$，使 $\gamma w\in F$ 的 $\gamma\in\Gamma$ 只有有限个。固定上游的复数步骤和 $p$ 进步骤均将该格子证书传给 `commensurator_superrigidity`。

Let $x,y,z$ be positive real numbers with $x^2+y^2+z^2=xyz$. Let $\Gamma$ be the subgroup of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ generated by the normal form matrices `nA` and `nB`. The induced topology on $\Gamma$ is discrete by `discreteTopology_normal`. Assume also $x<y+z$, $y<x+z$, and $z<x+y$. By `exists_strip_cover_normal`, there is one $\eta>0$ for all points of the upper half-plane. For each such point $w$, some $\gamma\in\Gamma$ satisfies $-1\le\Re(\gamma w)\le1$ and $\Im(\gamma w)\ge\eta$.

With these triangle inequalities, `goodLattice_normal` gives `GoodLattice Γ`. This property requires discreteness and a measurable set $F$ of finite hyperbolic area. Each orbit meets $F$. For each $w$, only finitely many $\gamma\in\Gamma$ satisfy $\gamma w\in F$. In the pinned upstream proof, the complex and $p$-adic steps both pass this lattice result to `commensurator_superrigidity`.
