## ProjectiveLine

本地模块以 $[x:1]$ 和 $[1:0]$ 表示有限点与无穷远点，并证明齐次矩阵公式与 Mathlib 在射影直线 $\mathbb P^1(k)$（`OnePoint k`）上的 $\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$ 作用相同。两个可逆矩阵在三个互异点处分别给出相同的像时，它们在整条射影直线上的作用相同。在非平凡赋范且闭球紧致的域上，对点的作用连续，矩阵参数在可逆值处变化时对固定点的作用也连续。`BoundaryMeasure` 用到这两种连续性，`Unipotent` 用参数连续性处理复平移，`CrossRatio` 用齐次括号和二次式研究交比。

The module uses $[x:1]$ for a finite point and $[1:0]$ for infinity. Its homogeneous matrix formula agrees with Mathlib's $\mathrm{GL}_2(k)$ action on `OnePoint k`. If two invertible matrices agree on three distinct points, they act identically on the projective line. Over a nontrivially normed field with compact closed balls, the action is continuous in the point. At an invertible value of a continuous matrix parameter, the action on a fixed point is continuous. `BoundaryMeasure` uses both continuity results. `Unipotent` uses continuity in the parameter for complex translations. `CrossRatio` uses the homogeneous bracket and quadratic forms to study cross ratios.

## ThreePoint

设 $e_3=(0,1,\infty)$，$h_t$ 是把 $e_3$ 送到互异三元组 $t$ 的射影变换。相对位置 $\mathrm{rel}(t,t')=h_t^{-1}t'$ 在 $t$ 互异时连续，并在两组点同时接受同一个射影变换时保持不变。$G_3(s,t)=h_sh_t^{-1}e_3$ 在 $s,t$ 都互异时连续。若 $t,t'$ 都是互异三元组，则 $G_3(t',\mathrm{rel}(t,t'))=t$。`FromProb` 由互异三元组集合的开放性推出其可测性。`TripleNull` 用相对位置控制紧集上的三元组，`PadicUnbounded` 用 $e_3$ 的轨道表述退化极限。

Let $e_3=(0,1,\infty)$. Let $h_t$ be the projective transformation that sends $e_3$ to the triple $t$ of distinct points. The relative position $\mathrm{rel}(t,t')=h_t^{-1}t'$ is continuous where $t$ has distinct points. It is invariant under the simultaneous projective action on both triples. The map $G_3(s,t)=h_sh_t^{-1}e_3$ is continuous where both triples have distinct points. It satisfies $G_3(t',\mathrm{rel}(t,t'))=t$ when both inputs to $\mathrm{rel}$ have distinct points. `FromProb` uses openness to obtain measurability of the set of distinct triples. `TripleNull` uses relative positions to control triples in compact sets. `PadicUnbounded` formulates a degenerate limit using the orbit of $e_3$.

## TailConvex

实 Hilbert 空间中任一有界序列 $x_k$ 都有收敛的尾部凸组合 $\sum_{k\ge n}w_{nk}x_k$。每行权重非负、有限支撑且和为一。在第 $n$ 个尾部凸包中取范数距下确界小于 $1/(n+1)$ 的点，再用平行四边形恒等式证明这些点组成 Cauchy 序列。`FurstenbergLimit` 对测试函数的归一化积分组成的 $L^2$ 序列应用此结论。

Every bounded sequence $x_k$ in a real Hilbert space admits norm-convergent tail combinations $\sum_{k\ge n}w_{nk}x_k$. Each row of weights has finite support, nonnegative entries, and sum one. Choose a point in each tail hull with norm within $1/(n+1)$ of the infimum. The parallelogram law makes this sequence Cauchy. `FurstenbergLimit` applies the result to an $L^2$ sequence of normalized test integrals.

実 Hilbert 空間の任意の有界列 $x_k$ に対し、ノルム収束する末尾の凸結合 $\sum_{k\ge n}w_{nk}x_k$ が存在する。各行の重みは非負で、台は有限であり、和は一である。第 $n$ の末尾凸包から、ノルムがその下限に $1/(n+1)$ 以内で近い点を取る。平行四辺形等式により、選んだ点の列は Cauchy 列になる。`FurstenbergLimit` はこの結果を、試験関数の正規化した積分からなる $L^2$ 列に使う。 [Local Hilbert-space proof](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftTailConvex.lean#L70) · [Local application in $L^2$](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftFurstenbergLimit.lean#L332)

## FurstenbergLimit

设 $\mu$ 是 $X$ 上的有限测度，$Y$ 是第二可数、紧致、可度量化的 Borel 空间。若对每个 $n$ 和每个有界连续实函数 $f$，$\Theta_n(x)\in\operatorname{Prob}(Y)$ 的 $f$ 积分关于 $x$ 可测，则存在与 $x$ 无关的尾权，使加权概率测度在 $\mu$ 几乎处处弱收敛。在不收敛的点赋一个固定概率测度，所得极限映射仍可测。若第 $k$ 项的一个测试积分误差至多为 $C/(k+1)$，第 $n$ 个尾部凸组合的误差至多为 $C/(n+1)$。`Furstenberg` 对实边界坐标上的高斯概率测度应用此结果，并用误差界把右侧上三角作用下的近似不变性传到极限。再由左侧格作用的等变性得到边界上的 Markov 核。

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $X$. Let $Y$ be a compact, metrizable, second-countable space with its Borel structure. For every $n$ and bounded continuous real $f$, assume that the integral of $f$ against $\Theta_n(x)\in\operatorname{Prob}(Y)$ is measurable in $x$. A single sequence of tail weights gives weak convergence for $\mu$-almost every $x$. A fixed probability measure on the exceptional set gives a measurable limit map. If the $k$th test integral error is at most $C/(k+1)$, the $n$th tail combination has error at most $C/(n+1)$. `Furstenberg` applies this result with a Gaussian probability measure on the real boundary chart. The error bound gives invariance of the limit under the right action of the upper triangular subgroup. Left lattice equivariance then gives a boundary Markov kernel.

$\mu$ を $X$ 上の有限測度とし、$Y$ を第二可算で距離化可能なコンパクト空間とする。$Y$ には Borel 構造を入れる。各 $n$ と各有界連続実関数 $f$ について、$\Theta_n(x)\in\operatorname{Prob}(Y)$ による $f$ の積分が $x$ に関して可測とする。$x$ に依存しない一組の末尾の重みにより、結合した確率測度は $\mu$ についてほとんど至る所で弱収束する。例外集合に固定した確率測度を割り当てた極限写像は可測である。第 $k$ 項の試験積分の誤差が $C/(k+1)$ 以下ならば、第 $n$ の末尾の凸結合の誤差は $C/(n+1)$ 以下である。`Furstenberg` は境界の実座標上でガウス確率測度を使ってこの結果を適用する。誤差評価から上三角群の右作用に関する極限の不変性を得て、格子の左作用に関する同変性から境界の Markov 核を得る。 [Local convergence and measurability](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftFurstenbergLimit.lean#L279) · [Local error bound](../../../../Solutions/Source_ReferenceIutCanLiftFurstenbergLimit.lean#L119) · [Pinned right invariance](https://github.com/lana-agents/orbicurve-cores/blob/61616dcd77fbe45c083a8ebfa7cc80728f86e841/OrbicurveCores/M2/Furstenberg.lean#L490) · [Pinned boundary kernel](https://github.com/lana-agents/orbicurve-cores/blob/61616dcd77fbe45c083a8ebfa7cc80728f86e841/OrbicurveCores/M2/Furstenberg.lean#L622)

## BoundaryMeasure

将密度 $(1+x^2)^{-1}$ 定义的有限 Cauchy 测度 $\nu$ 推前到 $B$，得到与 $\mathrm{bdry}$ 互相绝对连续的测度。对 $g\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ 和可测集 $E$，有 $\nu(g^{-1}E)\le\|g\|_{\mathrm F}^2\nu(E)$。因此每个群元素保持边界的 Lebesgue 测度类。对紧致可度量化 Borel 空间 $Y$、可测 $\psi:B\to Y$ 和收敛序列 $g_n\to g_0$，存在严格递增的 $n_i$，使 $\psi(g_{n_i}x)\to\psi(g_0x)$ 对 $\mathrm{bdry}$ 几乎处处成立。`ErgodicUse` 用拟保测性建立乘积上的对应结论，`Superrigid` 用子列提取在三个互异像点上确定延拓。

Push the finite Cauchy measure with density $(1+x^2)^{-1}$ to $B$, and call the result $\nu$. This measure and $\mathrm{bdry}$ are mutually absolutely continuous. For $g\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb R)$ and measurable $E$, the bound is $\nu(g^{-1}E)\le\|g\|_{\mathrm F}^2\nu(E)$. Thus each group element preserves the boundary Lebesgue measure class. Let $Y$ be a compact metrizable Borel space and let $\psi:B\to Y$ be measurable. If $g_n\to g_0$, a subsequence satisfies $\psi(g_{n_i}x)\to\psi(g_0x)$ for $\mathrm{bdry}$-almost every $x$. `ErgodicUse` uses this property to preserve the product measure class. `Superrigid` uses the subsequence to determine the extension from three distinct images.

## ErgodicUse

设 $\Gamma$ 可数且在 $B$ 上双遍历。将 $B^2$ 上一个几乎不变的可测集的全部群元素原像作可数交，可得与原集几乎处处相同的严格不变集。因此原集零测或余零测。对非空且可数分离的可测值域，几乎不变的可测映射几乎处处常值。将 $E\subset B$ 替换成 $E\times B$ 可得到单边的集合和映射结论。`TripleNull` 应用常值结论处理互异三点的质量和相对位置的质量，`FromProb` 用单边常值控制两点支持上的权重。`PointMaps` 用乘积的零测或余零测结论排除点映射的碰撞，`NoPair` 用乘积结论排除等变点对，`Superrigid` 用单边结论证明等变点映射唯一。

Assume $\Gamma$ is countable and doubly ergodic on $B$. For an almost invariant measurable set in $B^2$, intersect its preimages under all group elements. This gives a strictly invariant set equal to the original set almost everywhere. The original set is therefore null or conull. An almost invariant measurable map into a nonempty, countably separated measurable space is almost everywhere constant. The set and map results on $B$ follow by applying the product result to $E\times B$. `TripleNull` uses essential constancy for the mass of distinct triples and for the mass of relative positions. `FromProb` uses constancy on $B$ to control the weights of a two-point support. `PointMaps` uses the product dichotomy to rule out collisions almost everywhere. `NoPair` uses the product results to exclude equivariant pairs. `Superrigid` uses the dichotomy on $B$ to prove uniqueness of an equivariant point map.
