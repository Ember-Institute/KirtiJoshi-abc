# A counterexample on the concrete P7 data

## English

Fix the degree-one family $x_m=5^m/(2\cdot5^m+1)$ and its compactly bounded subset with $V=\{2\}$ and $c=\log 3$. Given a curve provider, core finiteness on this subset and the anabelian existence input, the theorem selects a family member and an admissible prime $\ell\ge 7$. Core finiteness and Northcott exclude finitely many parameters. The prime selection and cyclic subgroup estimates supply the conditions for the $\mathrm{SL}_2$ image criterion.

The contribution above $5$ gives $\log Q\ge 2m\log 5$. For $m\ge 6$ this exceeds $(126/11)\log\pi$, the upper bound implied by the concrete volume comparison. The selected P7 data therefore satisfy $\mathrm{rhs}<\mathrm{qPilot.lhs}$. Here $\mathrm{rhs}$ measures the principal-region hull assembled by the concrete local theory.
