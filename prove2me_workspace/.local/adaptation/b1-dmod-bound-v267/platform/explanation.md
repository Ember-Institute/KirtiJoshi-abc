Let $L=\mathbb Q(\lambda)\subseteq F_\lambda$. The Legendre formula gives

$$
j(E_\lambda)=\frac{256(\lambda^2-\lambda+1)^3}{\lambda^2(\lambda-1)^2}\in L.
$$

It follows that $M_\lambda=\mathbb Q(j(E_\lambda))$ is a subfield of $L$. The generator $\lambda$ is algebraic over $\mathbb Q$, so $L$ is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb Q$.

The embedding of $F_\lambda$ into $\overline{\mathbb Q}$ gives an isomorphism from $L$ onto the field generated by $\lambda$ in $\overline{\mathbb Q}$. Its degree is the degree $d_\lambda$ of the minimal polynomial. The inclusion of $M_\lambda$ in $L$ now gives

$$
[M_\lambda:\mathbb Q]\le [L:\mathbb Q]=d_\lambda.
$$
