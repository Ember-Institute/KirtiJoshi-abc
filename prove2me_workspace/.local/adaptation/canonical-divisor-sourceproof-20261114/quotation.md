> Write $D \overset{\mathrm{def}}{=} \sum_{t\in T}[t]$ for the effective divisor on X obtained by taking the formal sum of the points in T, each with multiplicity one; denote the associated line bundle $\mathcal{O}_X(D)$ by $\mathcal{L}$ and the canonical bundle of X by $\omega_X$.

— Shinichi Mochizuki, *Noncritical Belyi Maps*, proof of Theorem 2.5, printed p.6.
